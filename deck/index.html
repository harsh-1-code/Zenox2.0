The problem

The guidance exists. It just isn’t there when it matters.

Digital payments reached Madhya Pradesh faster than the knowledge to use them safely. Fraud now arrives through the same channels people use every day.

At the moment of decisionNo reliable way to assess a message, call or app prompt — in the 40 seconds that matter.
After money has goneMost victims do not know 1930 exists, or that a transfer reported in the first hour can sometimes be held.
Shame delays reportingBy the time someone admits it, recovery is no longer possible.
What we built

Not a classifier. A decision assistant.

“We are not asking people to trust another AI verdict. We show the evidence, the uncertainty, how to verify it independently, and what to do next.”

Scam DNAStructured attack pattern — impersonation, urgency, requested action, tactics — not a label.
Legitimacy challengeActively looks for evidence the message is genuine. This is the false-alarm defence.
Stated uncertaintyA fourth state: UNCERTAIN. Unknowns are shown, never hidden behind confidence.
One targeted questionWhen evidence is thin, it asks the single question that changes the advice.

How it works

One Claude call. Deterministic where it matters.

Message / screenshot / call / app→ Redact secrets→ One Claude call→ Validate + safety guards→ Deterministic action plan→ Session wiped
Multimodal, one callA six-prompt chain measured 130s. One call returns the whole assessment in ~8s.
Actions are code, not output1930 and NCRP steps come from a Python table, so guidance always matches published procedure.
Live verification, on demand“Research this live” uses Claude’s web search to find the bank’s real domain — and cites every page.

Safety & privacy

The rules are code, not prompt text.

Seven guards, one test eachNever elicits an OTP or PIN · no high risk without evidence · no uncalibrated percentage · no invented source · “not found” never becomes “fraud” · no clickable suspicious link · nothing raw reaches the logs.
Zero retention, enforcedSessions live in memory with a 30-minute TTL. Secrets are stripped before the Claude call, all PII before any log line. The service worker caches the shell and never an assessment. A Clear session button proves it live.
Advisory onlyNo automatic reporting, no contact with banks or police on anyone’s behalf. Every factual claim resolves to a source with a date.
Works in the user’s languageHindi and English throughout, including the hand-translated action tables — not machine-translated at runtime.
What we deliberately did not do

Two things we could have faked.

No “92% scam” scoreA precise-looking number with no calibrated evaluation behind it is invented confidence. Our gauge encodes four interpretable states, and a guard strips percentages from model output.
No fabricated RBI listRBI’s DLA directory is a JavaScript viewer with no fetchable export. Rather than ship invented rows we say “directory not loaded”, link the real page, and fall back to cited live research. A one-command importer is ready.

In a product whose job is exposing false authority, shipping false authority would disqualify it.
